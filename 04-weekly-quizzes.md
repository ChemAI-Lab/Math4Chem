Weekly quizzes are posted here.

# Instructions 

A new list of quizzes will be provided each tutorial. These exercises are to be completed individually and are due by the end of the next tutorial session. 

All quizzes must be submitted electronically by email to decamara@mcmaster.ca. You must use the following naming template for your submission file: STUDENT_SURNAME_STUDENT_ID_QUIZ_NUMBER.pdf (e.g., SMITH_001234567_QUIZ_2.pdf). Files not written in this proper format may not receive credit. In the subject of the email write: 3PC3 QUIZ QUIZ_NUMBER.   

<b>Alternative submission:</b> To the teaching assistant (monitor) by the end of the tutorial section in which they are due. Be sure that you write your full name and student number on the cover page. Submissions without identification will not be accepted.
